Who we are

Voice of Disability is a South African, disability-led nonprofit company founded to amplify disabled women's voices about the socio-economic matters affecting their daily lives. We champion their rights and participation, and engage decision-makers to address disabling barriers.

Our work brings together advocacy, independent living, Universal Design, accessible information and opportunities for skills development and economic inclusion.

Our vision and mission

Vision

A society in which disabled women have the voice, access, independence and opportunities to participate equally in every aspect of life, influence decisions that affect them, and shape their own futures.

Mission

Voice of Disability amplifies disabled women's voices, advances their rights and works to remove barriers to full participation in society. Through accessible digital platforms, advocacy, Universal Design, independent living initiatives and inclusive education and economic opportunity, we enable disabled women to speak up, speak out and participate meaningfully in mainstream society.

Our strategic priorities

Voice and advocacy

Support disabled women to express their experiences, engage decision-makers and advocate for changes to policies, services and society.

Access and independent living

Advance Personal Assistance Services, accessibility, Universal Design and the removal of barriers to community participation.

Digital skills and economic opportunity

Develop inclusive pathways to digital and AI literacy, employment and entrepreneurship, giving priority to disabled women without creating segregated training or employment.

These priorities describe our direction. Specific training programmes and partnerships are subject to resources and implementation arrangements; they should not be read as claims that every proposed service is currently available.

Our history: founded in 2010

Fadila Lagadien began Voice of Disability independently in 2010, building the initiative and undertaking member mobilisation to create a platform where disabled women could speak up and speak out about matters affecting their lives.

After six years of this foundational work, Voice of Disability was formally incorporated with South Africa's Companies and Intellectual Property Commission on 20 April 2016 as Voice of Disability NPC.

CIPC registration number: 2016/149549/08.

Our founding principle remains Nothing About Us Without Us.

Mainstream inclusion, not segregation

We prioritise disabled women while recognising that accessible programmes can benefit the wider community. We do not seek to create sheltered workshops, segregated training or sheltered employment as substitutes for equal participation in mainstream opportunities.

Our approach is rooted in the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, including the rights of women with disabilities, accessibility, independent living, freedom of expression and education.

Our advocacy role is distinct from any grant-funded programme that specifically restricts spending on lobbying or political activity.

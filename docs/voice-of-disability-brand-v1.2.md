## Approved logo
The approved working identity is **Concept A**: four rounded open-circle segments in Voice Navy, Leadership Plum, Justice Gold and Voice Rose. It is **not** a typographic-only identity. Do not recreate or replace the mark.

Source of truth: `public/brand/brand-manifest.json`.

## Website and Facebook rules
Use the official horizontal asset in website navigation and Facebook cover graphics. Use the approved compact mark in the Facebook profile picture. Protect the artwork from cropping; do not use a stylised wheelchair or megaphone as a substitute. On Facebook, upload a raster PNG derived from the original SVG, not a screenshot. Keep the page name and URL unchanged.

## Accessibility
Preserve useful alternative text for meaningful illustrations; verify keyboard access, mobile cropping, readable contrast and recognisability at small display sizes. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AAA where practical, with AA as the baseline. Facebook controls are third-party and cannot be certified from the graphic files alone.

VOICE OF DISABILITY — REASONABLE ACCOMMODATION REQUEST TEMPLATE
Editable plain-text version

Date: [Day Month Year]
To: [Name / Human Resources / Manager]
Organisation: [Organisation name]

Subject: Request for reasonable accommodation

Dear [Name],

I am requesting reasonable accommodation because I am experiencing a disability-related barrier affecting my ability to [describe the relevant task, process or part of participation].

The barrier I experience is: [describe the barrier in practical terms].

The adjustment that would help me participate on an equal basis is: [describe the accommodation you are requesting].

This adjustment would assist by: [briefly explain how it removes or reduces the barrier].

I am available to discuss my request and other suitable options if necessary. Please confirm receipt and let me know the next steps.

Please respond using [preferred communication method, if applicable].

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[Your preferred contact details]

BEFORE SENDING
- Keep the request focused on the barrier and proposed solution.
- Save a copy of your request and response.
- Use the organisation's formal process where practical.
- If refused, ask for the reasons and possible alternatives.

IMPORTANT
This general template is not legal advice. For disputes, dismissals or legal deadlines, seek appropriate professional advice.
